About Me

Aesthetics, Clarity &
Thoughtful Details

Hi, everyone! I am Alara, a 21-year-old International Business student from Cyprus with a strong interest in digital marketing, content creation, organisation management, and design. With my Bachelor's degree, I am studying International Business, specialising in Organisation and Change, also following a Digital Marketing Minor programme. Currently, I am doing my HR&Branding Internship at a telecommunications company called Turkcell.

I am someone who values clarity, aesthetics, and thoughtful details. I love turning ideas into something clear, structured, and visually calm. I enjoy learning by doing, experimenting with ideas, and improving my work step by step. I am naturally curious and organised, and bringing structure to creative processes gives me satisfaction. I can describe myself as a really positive and hopeful person, who always thinks there will be solutions no matter what the problems are.

This website is a small reflection of how I think and work. It is a space where I share my projects, interests, and the things I am learning along the way!
